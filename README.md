# Last-Song-of-Elysian
Last Song of Elysian is a Minecraft Forge mod inspired by the Elysian Realm from Honkai Impact 3rd. It brings Flame-Chasers, Signet-based character building, Herrscher Cores, curses, and trials into Minecraft’s exploration and combat.
Players can travel to a dedicated Elysian Realm dimension, interact with Flame-Chasers such as Pardofelis and Vill-V, purchase items from Pardofelis’s Shop, and fuse or upgrade Signets through the Helix Workshop. Each Signet provides unique combat or utility effects involving damage, defence, healing, energy, combos, evasion, elemental abilities, and other special mechanics.
The mod also introduces the Cocoon of Finality and Herrscher Core system. Cores can be collected in any order, gradually increasing the power of the Cocoon’s curses. After gathering the required Cores, players can face twelve Trials of Finality. Completing each trial removes its corresponding curse and unlocks a blessing, eventually leading to the final reversal of the Cocoon.
Additional content includes Remembrance Sigils, Domain of Ego weapons and abilities, custom Flame-Chaser models, particles and animations, combat HUDs, special enemies, Soulium materials, the Silver Pouch, and a music playback system. Gameplay data and major effects are synchronized for multiplayer.

乐土终曲是一款以《崩坏3》“往世乐土”为灵感制作的 Minecraft Forge 模组，尝试将英桀、刻印构筑、律者核心与乐土试炼融入 Minecraft 的探索和战斗之中。
玩家可以进入独立的往世乐土维度，与帕朵菲莉丝、维尔薇等英桀互动，通过帕朵商店购买物品，并在螺旋工坊中融合、升级刻印。不同刻印拥有各自的战斗与辅助效果，涵盖伤害、防御、治疗、能量、连击、闪避、元素能力和特殊机制，让玩家可以组合出不同的成长路线。
模组还加入了终焉之茧与律者核心系统。玩家可以按照任意顺序收集核心，逐步强化诅咒，并在集齐所需核心后开启十二项终焉试炼。完成试炼可以解除对应诅咒并获得祝福，最终完成终焉之茧的反转。
除此之外，模组还包含追忆之证、真我之境系列武器与技能、专属英桀模型、粒子动画、战斗 HUD、特殊怪物、魂钢材料、银币袋以及音乐播放系统。相关能力与数据支持多人游戏同步。
